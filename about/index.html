About GoZapTools

Free online tools, built to respect your privacy.

GoZapTools is a collection of free online tools designed to make everyday tasks faster and simpler. Whether you need to create an invoice, generate a payslip, build a CV, or convert units, our tools get the job done in seconds — with no signup, no login, and no cost.

What we believe

Most "free" online tools come with a catch: forced signups, watermarks on your downloads, or your data being collected and sold. We built GoZapTools to be different.

  • Genuinely free — every tool is free to use with no hidden charges or premium paywalls
  • No watermarks — your downloads are clean and professional, with no branding stamped on them
  • Privacy-first — our tools run entirely in your browser, so your data never leaves your device
  • No account needed — open a tool and start using it immediately

How our tools work

Every GoZapTools tool runs using JavaScript directly in your web browser. When you create an invoice or generate a payslip, all the processing happens on your own device. Nothing is uploaded to a server, nothing is stored in a database, and nothing is logged. When you close the tab, your data is gone.

This browser-based approach means your sensitive information — salary figures, client details, financial data — stays completely private. We literally cannot see what you create.

Who we are

GoZapTools is an independent project built and maintained by a small team passionate about creating useful, accessible tools for everyone. We're supported by non-intrusive advertising, which allows us to keep every tool free forever.

Our tools

We currently offer 8 free tools including an invoice generator, salary slip generator, Base64 encoder/decoder, CV builder, electricity bill calculator, loan EMI calculator, cooking unit converter, and WhatsApp chat maker. We're always adding more.
