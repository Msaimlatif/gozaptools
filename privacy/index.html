Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

At GoZapTools, your privacy is fundamental to how we build our tools. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it's used, and the choices you have. By using our website, you agree to the practices described here.

The short version

Our tools run entirely in your browser. The data you enter — invoice details, salary figures, personal information — is never sent to our servers, never stored, and never shared. It stays on your device.

Information you enter into tools

When you use a tool like the invoice generator or payslip maker, the information you type is processed entirely within your web browser using JavaScript. This data is never transmitted to us or any third party. We have no database of user-entered content and no ability to access what you create.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website — which tools are popular, how people navigate, and where we can improve. Google Analytics collects standard, anonymised information such as:

  • Pages visited and time spent on the site
  • General geographic region (country or city level)
  • Device type, browser, and operating system
  • How you arrived at our site (search engine, direct, referral)

This data is aggregated and anonymous. It does not identify you personally and is never linked to the content you enter into our tools. You can learn more in Google's Privacy Policy.

Cookies

We use a small number of cookies, primarily for analytics (via Google Analytics) and, in the future, to remember your preferences such as favourite tools. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. Our tools will continue to work without them.

Advertising

GoZapTools may display advertisements to keep our tools free. If we use Google AdSense or similar networks, these partners may use cookies to serve relevant ads. You can manage ad personalisation through Google Ad Settings.

Third-party links

Our site may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies.

Children's privacy

Our tools are intended for general audiences and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our contact page.
