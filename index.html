About Our Tools

Free Payslip Generator — For Any Business, Anywhere

Are you looking for a free tool to generate a salary slip online? You've found it. GoZapTools Payslip Generator lets you create a professional, printable salary slip in under 2 minutes — completely free, with no account required.

Whether you run a small business in the UK, a startup in the UAE, a company in the USA, or a private firm in Pakistan, our tool covers you. Select your currency, enter the employee's details, add earnings and deductions, and download a clean A4 PDF payslip instantly.

  • Supports USD, GBP, EUR, AED, PKR, CAD and more
  • All standard allowances: HRA, medical, transport, overtime
  • All deduction types: tax, insurance, loan, pension
  • Blank fields are automatically hidden in the PDF
  • No watermark, no branding on your payslip

Electricity Bill Calculator — Know Before It Arrives

Tired of being surprised by your electricity bill? Our free electricity bill calculator lets you estimate your monthly bill before it arrives at your door. Enter your units consumed and get a full breakdown in seconds.

The calculator uses officially approved tariff rates and includes all components that appear on your real bill — energy charges by slab, fuel cost adjustment, GST, electricity duty, and meter rent. No guesswork, just accurate estimates.

  • Full slab-by-slab breakdown
  • Includes taxes, surcharges and fixed charges
  • Supports residential and commercial connections
  • Highlights which slab your usage falls in
  • Shows effective cost per unit consumed
