Are You Looking for a Free Payslip Generator?

GoZapTools Salary Slip Generator is the easiest way to create a professional payslip online — completely free. Whether you're a small business owner, a freelancer, a startup, or an HR manager, you can generate a clean, printable salary slip in under 2 minutes.

No software to install. No account to create. No watermark on your download. Just open the tool, fill in the details, and click download.

Our payslip generator supports multiple currencies including USD, GBP, EUR, AED, SAR, PKR, INR, CAD, AUD and more. Whether you're processing payroll in the USA, UK, UAE, Pakistan, or anywhere else — this tool works for you.

  • Blank fields are hidden automatically in the PDF
  • Download a clean A4 payslip — no watermark
